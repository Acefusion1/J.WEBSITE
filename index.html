Our story

The UJC was opened in 2014, when families across Durban were moving north to Umhlanga. The idea was simple: bring the essentials of Jewish life onto one campus, so a family could daven, learn, school their children and celebrate together in one place.

Today the shul seats up to 350 people, and the centre is busy every day of the week, from early-morning minyan to youth programmes in the afternoon.
