About us

A Modern Orthodox shul, school and mikveh, side by side on Izinga Ridge since 2014.

Our story

As more Durban families settled north in Umhlanga, the community needed a home closer by. The UJC opened its doors in 2014 with one goal: put the shul, the school and the mikveh side by side, so Jewish life could happen in one place.

The shul now holds up to 350 people. On any given day you'll find a morning minyan, kids heading to class and something happening for the youth after school.
